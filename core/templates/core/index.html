📖 CTC Knowledge Base

What is the difference between CTC and Gross Salary?

CTC (Cost to Company) is the total amount the company spends on an employee, including Gross Salary, employer contributions (like PF and Gratuity), and perks. Gross Salary is the total earnings before any deductions are made.

What is Basic Salary and why is it important?

Basic Salary is the core component of the salary, typically 40% to 50% of the CTC. It is important because many other components, like HRA and Provident Fund (PF), are calculated as a percentage of the Basic Salary.

What is HRA (House Rent Allowance)?

HRA is an allowance given by the employer to cover the employee's rental expenses. It is usually 40% of Basic Salary for non-metro cities and 50% for metro cities. It also offers significant tax exemptions.

What are Employer Contributions?

These are amounts the company pays on behalf of the employee, such as Employer PF, Gratuity, and insurance premiums. They are included in the CTC but are not paid directly to the employee's bank account.

What are Deductions?

Deductions are amounts subtracted from the Gross Salary to arrive at the Net Take-Home Salary. Common deductions include Employee PF, Professional Tax, and Income Tax (TDS).

What is the difference between Fixed Pay and Variable Pay?

Fixed Pay (like Basic Salary and HRA) is guaranteed and paid every month. Variable Pay (like Performance Bonuses or Incentives) is not guaranteed and depends on individual or company performance, usually paid annually or quarterly.

What is Gratuity?

Gratuity is a lump sum amount paid by the employer to an employee as a reward for long-term service. It is typically calculated as 4.81% of the Basic Salary and is payable only after the employee completes 5 years of continuous service.

How is Net Take-Home Salary calculated?

Net Take-Home Salary is calculated by taking the Gross Salary and subtracting all applicable Deductions (like PF, Tax, and Professional Tax).

What are Perks and Benefits?

These are non-cash or reimbursement-based benefits provided to employees, such as food coupons, internet reimbursements, or gym memberships. They add to the CTC but may have specific tax rules.

What is Professional Tax?

Professional Tax is a state-level tax levied on employment. The amount varies by state and is usually a small fixed amount deducted monthly from the employee's salary.
